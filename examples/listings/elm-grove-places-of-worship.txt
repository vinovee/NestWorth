The neighbourhood
Elm Grove is a quiet cul-de-sac in a close-knit, multicultural neighbourhood. Within half a mile there are two churches, a mosque, a gurdwara and a Hindu temple, along with a parade of independent shops, a GP surgery and Sampleford Park. Sampleford station is a 12-minute walk, with trains to Manchester Piccadilly every 15 minutes at peak times (about 24 minutes).
