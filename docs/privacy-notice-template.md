## How [service name] uses your information

**Who we are.** [Name and contact details of the person or organisation running the service] is the controller of your personal data. [Contact details of your data protection officer, if you have one.]

**What the service does.** It helps you assess homes for sale against your own criteria using AI, and keeps your shortlist.

**What stays on your device.** Your profile (including any sale price, mortgage balance and savings you enter), your homes, notes and ratings are stored in the web browser on your device. We don't receive them. You can export or delete them at any time on the Profile page.

**What we process, and why.** When you ask for an assessment, our server receives the listing (its text, or a link that the server reads for you) and your search criteria: where you'll commute to, your budget figures, the types of home you want and any notes you write for the AI. It doesn't receive your sale price, mortgage balance or savings. Phone numbers and email addresses in the listing are removed before it's sent to the AI provider. [If web research is on: the AI provider also searches the web for public information about the home and its area.]

[Describe the safeguard for the transfer, for example the UK International Data Transfer Addendum or the UK Extension to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.] [Hosting provider, if any.]

**How long we keep it.** Our server doesn't store listings, criteria or assessments. Its logs record technical details (time, duration, token counts and error codes), not the listing or your criteria, and are kept for [period]. [The AI provider keeps what it receives for [period] under its terms.]

**Our lawful basis.** [For example, legitimate interests, or the performance of a contract, depending on your relationship with users.]

**AI and automated decisions.** Assessments are generated by AI and labelled as such. They can be wrong. They're information to help you, not decisions about you: you can correct any fact and ignore any verdict. They aren't mortgage, financial or legal advice.
